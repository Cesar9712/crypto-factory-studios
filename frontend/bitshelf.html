FAQ

Good to know

Which crypto do you accept?

BitShelf checkout uses native USDC on Base only.

Do I connect my wallet?

No. We never ask for your seed phrase or private key. You send USDC from your own wallet and provide the public transaction hash.

When do I receive the files?

After the backend verifies the network, token contract, recipient, amount and confirmations.

Can I share or resell the pack?

You may use and adapt resources in your own work and client projects, but you may not redistribute or resell the original files as a competing pack.
