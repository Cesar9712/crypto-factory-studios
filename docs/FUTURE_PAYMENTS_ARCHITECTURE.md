# Future payments architecture — BLOCKED PHASE

Status: **DO NOT IMPLEMENT NOW**.

Real-money systems are intentionally postponed until the core game, accounts, persistence, economy and marketplace demo are stable.

The following remain disabled in this phase:

- deposits and crypto purchases
- fiat payments
- withdrawals / cash-out
- play-to-earn settlement
- real token purchases or sales
- treasury custody
- real marketplace settlement
- bridges and exchanges
- real-money rewards

Before any future implementation, perform a separate design/security/legal review covering:

1. supported jurisdictions and product eligibility;
2. KYC/AML obligations where applicable;
3. custody vs non-custodial architecture;
4. chain and token selection;
5. deposit finality and reorg handling;
6. withdrawal queues, limits and abuse controls;
7. fee/gas policy;
8. treasury key management and multisig;
9. smart-contract audit and emergency pause;
10. accounting, reconciliation and transaction history;
11. sanctions/blocked-address controls where required;
12. fraud, chargeback and account-takeover response.

Only local/testnet contract experiments are allowed before this phase is explicitly unlocked.
